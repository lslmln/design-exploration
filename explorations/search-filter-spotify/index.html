Gap flagged: Spotify's own filter-chip and search-bar specs exist (§4 of its DESIGN.md — this pattern is a genuine match, not an improvisation), but Spotify's app has no native "list of results with a leading thumbnail and trailing rating/price" component — its closest real primitive is the Track Row (56pt, thumbnail + stacked title/artist). Reused that row's structure and metrics here for the result cards, since it's the nearest documented pattern, but Spotify never shows ratings, prices, or availability chips inline like this — the row content itself is invented for this comparison, only its shell and metrics are real.
